# Billing Workflows

All billing graphs are defined in code in `.seeds/billing/` (registry `billingGraphs` in `index.ts`, one file per area: `facts`, `service`, `charges`, `claim`, `checks`, `facility`, `documents`, `intake`, `cycle`, `payer-files`, `recovery`, `schedules`) and seeded as system graphs, in dependency order. Every billing decision is a small **rule** graph or a keyed **table**; **stages** sequence rules, actions and tasks; **workflows** react to an event. Code nodes only read and write (facts, storage, X12, the model) and never decide. Every pin and exit of every seeded graph has a Title Case label and a description: the seed runs `documentationIssues` next to `validateGraph` and fails on either. Pins the glossary (`.seeds/billing/glossary.ts`) knows are documented once there.

## Encounter Facts

`Map Encounter Facts` (stage) builds one typed **Encounter Facts** model (`billing.encounter-facts`) from the PCR, once per report, with Break and Make nodes like every model. Every later rule reads facts, never NEMSIS sections; task details (Review PCR, Review Updated PCR, Trip Summary) still show the report itself.

| Section       | Model                    | Rule                     | Holds                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ------------- | ------------------------ | ------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| trip          | `billing.trip-facts`     | `billing.trip-facts`     | PCR number, date of service, call time, type of service, response mode, dispositions, level of care, cardiac arrest outcome (eArrest.18), CMS service level, ALS assessment, odometers                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| locations     | `billing.location-facts` | `billing.location-facts` | pickup and destination, incident location type, destination type, main reason for the destination                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| patient       | `billing.demographics`   | Demographics From PCR    | name, birth date, sex, address                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| clinical      | `billing.clinical-facts` | `billing.clinical-facts` | impressions, coded medications with routes, coded procedures, narrative                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| documentation | `billing.documentation`  | Documentation Facts      | signature, certification, the transport reason as reported, conditions, prior authorization, weight, times, patients transported (eDisposition.11), miles to the closest hospital (ePayment.48), the MSP accident answer (**MSP From the Report** table: workers compensation as the payment method or a work-related injury, a transport accident as the cause of injury). ABN, expected denial, the other MSP answers, PA expiry, PCS type, round trips, admission date and facility stay start empty and are entered by a biller |
| coverages     | `billing.coverage` list  | Coverages From PCR       | insurances as reported, or the payer the payment method names (Payer by Payment Method table)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |

The **Agency Facts** slot runs last.

## Intake: PCR received to Ready for review

```
read report → open draft claim
├ duplicate → Review Updated PCR (maps the facts, classifies) → Work the Claim Again | close
└ Work Draft Claim
   ├ Map Encounter Facts
   ├ Decide Whether to Claim: Classify Trip (Trip Type table → Agency Trip Type) → no-claim trips ask a biller
   ├ Read Narrative Findings (local model, with quotes)
   ├ Claim Patient (match, confirm, assign)
   ├ Determine Payers: Match Payer Directory → Medicare Secondary Payer → Coordination Order → Agency Payer Rules
   └ Bill the Claim
      ├ Determine Service: Emergency | Scheduled | Treat No Transport | Refusal → Deceased Handling (QL) → Agency Service
      │    each: Base Service = Level of Service + Emergency Status + Loaded Miles + Non-Covered Miles
      ├ Review PCR (coding review task, every reason shown)
      ├ Build Claim: Assign Charges → Assign Claim Properties → Agency Claim Rules → Save → Check Eligibility
      ├ Decide Responsibility: Facility Responsibility on the saved claim
      │    facility → Bill Facility (task Bill facility: bill the facility → claims.facility_id, or bill the payers)
      ├ Fix Claim (payers): Check Claim → Fix Claim Data task → Patch → Reprice Triggers → Reprice Claim
      └ Start Payer Cycle (a facility claim waits on the facility step for its invoice, no 837P)
```

### Checks

`Claim Checks` (`billing.claim-checks`) runs every check rule on the **Check Facts** (`billing.check-facts`, read by `billing.read-claim-checks`, which also does the date math: days a certification was signed before the trip and days left to file). Each check returns issues `{ code, field, message, coverageId }`; the **Check Severity** lookup table (`billing.check-severity`) gives each code its severity (`blocking`, `warning` or `off`; a code not listed blocks), so an agency downgrades, upgrades or turns off a check with one row. The **Agency Checks** slot adds or drops issues before severities apply. The Fix Claim Data form asks the fields each issue's `field` names.

## Claim cycle

`Cycle Claim` loops: **Read Claim Ledger** (action, `billing.read-claim-ledger`) states where the claim stands as a typed **Claim Ledger** (balance, the payer being billed and whether it has the claim or approved it, a response waiting to be reconciled, patient approval, statements and the time since each, why the last submission was rejected). **Next Claim Step** (rule) decides the step with the **Claim Policy** table (by the kind of payer billed, its claim filing indicator or `patient`: days to wait for a payer, statements to send and days between them, collections threshold, auto-write-off limit, review required, and the write-off and collections approval thresholds) and the **Claim Steps** table (top to bottom: overpaid, closed, work a denial, reconcile, facility responsible, review, submit, await, follow up, write off, review before the patient, confirm payers, wait between statements, statement, write off under the collections threshold, else collections). **Record Claim Step** stores the step's status (Status for the Step lookup) and continues through stale when the claim changed meanwhile. Steps the table marks `check` run Check Claim first (only patient issues before billing the patient, with the last rejection on review); a blocking issue sends the claim to Fix Claim. A step the cycle has no branch for (an overridden rule or Claim Steps row naming an unknown step) opens a `Claim step not handled` task instead of looping, and the cycle tries again once a biller retries.

| Step                  | Status                     | Subworkflow                                                                                                        |
| --------------------- | -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| review, reviewPatient | needs_review               | Review Claim                                                                                                       |
| confirmPayers         | needs_review               | Confirm Payers                                                                                                     |
| submit                | ready                      | Prepare 837P → Map 837P → File 837P → Send 837P                                                                    |
| await, awaitPatient   | submitted, patient_balance | Wait For Signal (claim changes or the deadline)                                                                    |
| followUp              | follow_up                  | Follow Up Payer: 276 (real time, or batch and wait up to a day for the 277), then a task showing the latest status |
| reconcile             | reconcile                  | Reconcile Payment                                                                                                  |
| workDenial            | denied                     | Work Denial (child run): Correct Claim or Appeal Claim (child runs), another payer, the patient or write off       |
| overpaid              | reconcile                  | Refund Overpayment (child run)                                                                                     |
| facility              | facility_billing           | Wait For Signal: the facility pays on its monthly invoice (Invoice Facilities)                                     |
| statement             | patient_balance            | Send Statement: Patient Responsibility first, then the statement for what the patient may be billed                |
| collections           | balance_review             | Collections Review task, then Approve Closing                                                                      |
| writeOff, closed      | —                          | Approve Closing for write-offs, then Close Claim as written off or paid                                            |

The base policy (30 days, 3 statements 30 days apart, no threshold, no auto-write-off, review required, write-offs above $500 and every collections placement approved) keeps the behavior of claims that are paid; a new row for a payer kind changes it for those payers only. **Approve Closing** (`billing.approve-closing`, with the **Closing Approval** rule) asks someone with `claims:approve` to approve writing off a balance above `writeOffApprovalCents` or sending one above `collectionsApprovalCents` to collections, and logs `closing_approved`; a rejection sends the claim back to the step that chose it.

## Payer files

`Receive Payer File` reads the file first (**Read Payer File**, no writes): its kind and, for an 835, each claim payment as a **Remittance Line** with the claim and payer it matched and the facts (denied, reversal, takeback, merged adjustments). **Route Remittances** runs **Route Remittance** per line: **Classify Adjustments** gives each adjustment a class by group code (**Adjustment Classes** table: CO and PI contractual, OA and CR other, PR patient responsibility; a denied claim lowers nothing) and adds up what lowers the balance; the **Remittance Routing** table (decision on the claim match, reversal, takeback, reason count, payer on the claim, denial, reason codes and classes) routes it: `reconcile` posts it and the cycle asks a biller to reconcile, `review` leaves it to a biller unposted, `unmatched` names no claim. **Apply Payer File** then applies the whole file once, posting each 835 line as routed; the data layer only persists. The `denial`, `appeal` and `fix` routes post the payment and log a `denied` event the claim cycle works (Work Denial).

## Denials, corrections and appeals

Remittance Routing posts a denial like any payment and also logs a `denied` event with its route: `fix` (CARC 4, 11, 16, 146, 181, 182), `appeal` (CARC 50, 56, 150, 151, 197) or `denial` (any other denied claim). While a denial waits (`denialRoute` on the ledger), Next Claim Step returns `workDenial` and the cycle runs **Work Denial** as a child run:

| Rule or task              | Key                           | Decides or asks                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ------------------------- | ----------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Denial Category           | `billing.denial-category`     | **Denial Categories** table: route and codes → suggested decision (correct, appeal, another payer, patient, write off)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| task Denial Decision      | in `billing.work-denial`      | Correct Claim, Appeal, Bill Another Payer (picks a directory payer, then `reconciled`), Move to Patient (`reconciled` and every remaining payer skipped), Write Off (Approve Closing)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Replacement or Reopening  | `billing.correction-method`   | **Correction Methods** table, by the payer directory's **Corrections** setting, else Medicare reopens and others replace: `replacement` queues `resubmit_queued` with the payer claim number and asks to fix the data, so the cycle reviews and files CLM05-3 = 7 with REF*F8; `void` files the 837P at once with CLM05-3 = 8 and REF*F8 and moves on from the payer; `reopening` asks a biller to request it and logs `reopening_requested` (the cycle then waits on the payer and follows up) |
| Appeal Level and Deadline | `billing.appeal-level`        | **Appeal Levels** table: Medicare redetermination 120 days and reconsideration 180 days from the remittance (then an ALJ hearing, _verify_); Medicaid 90 days and other payers 180 days unless the payer directory sets an **Appeal Limit**; with the days to wait for the decision                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| A Build Appeal Packet     | `billing.build-appeal-packet` | One PDF: request letter on the billing profile letterhead, claim summary, the patient care report and every PDF document on the claim; logged as `appeal_packet` in the claim files                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| task Send Appeal          | in `billing.appeal-claim`     | Due on the appeal deadline; sent logs `appealed`, then the run waits on claim signals up to the decision wait                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Appeal Decision           | `billing.appeal-decision`     | **Appeal Decisions** table: a remittance after the appeal is overturned, partial or upheld, logged as `appeal_decided`; with no answer in time a **Appeal Decision Overdue** task (keep waiting, upheld, withdrawn)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |

An upheld appeal leaves the denial open, so the next Denial Decision can appeal at the next level. Medicare reopenings and appeal levels are _verify_ items with the MAC.

## Records requests, refunds and balance-billing protection

- **Respond to Records Request** (`billing.respond-records-request`, from Records Request on the claim page): **Records Due** (**Records Deadlines** table: the date the request names, else Medicare 45 days and others 30, _verify_) logs `records_requested` and opens **Send Records**, due on that date; sent logs `records_sent`.
- **Refund Overpayment** (`billing.refund-overpayment`, the cycle's `overpaid` step): **Read Credit Balance**, **Refund Due** (**Refund Deadlines** table: Medicare and Medicaid within 60 days of being identified, 42 CFR 401.305; patients 30 days) and **Approve Refund**, which needs `claims:approve`; approval records a `refunded` event to whoever paid last, never more than they paid or than the credit balance, and keeping the credit closes the claim as paid. The step starts whenever the balance falls below zero: an 835 or a hand-posted payment that pays more than is owed is posted in full (its adjustment capped at what it left unpaid), leaving the credit to refund.
- **Patient Responsibility** (`billing.patient-responsibility`) runs in Send Patient Statement before each statement: **Read Patient Balance** (pickup state, date of service, last payer's plan type and kind, the PR cost share it stated) and the **Balance-Billing Protection** table: Medicaid blocks balance billing, Medicare caps it at the deductible and coinsurance, self-funded (ERISA) plans are exempt, and the example state rows cap fully insured plans at the in-network cost share in NH from 2026-01-01, OR from 2026-01-01 and AL from 2026-10-01 (all _verify_). **Protect Patient Balance** takes the protected amount off the balance as a `balance_protected` event with why; a blocked balance closes the claim with nothing billed.

## Deadlines and facilities

- **Watch Deadlines** runs every morning per agency: the worker checks `billing-watch-deadlines` with pg-boss every 15 minutes and starts one run for each active agency once its own day (in the billing profile time zone, UTC without one) has reached 6:00, recorded in `schedule_runs` (agency, workflow, day) so a redelivered or retried job never starts a second run and one failing agency does not stop the others. Every date the billing nodes use as today (deadlines, invoice and appeal packet dates, refunds, filing days left, eligibility and claim status requests) is the agency's date from **Agency Today** and the billing profile time zone, and a date-only task due date is the end of that day there. **Find Deadlines** lists timely filing for payers not filed yet (`filing_limit_days`, or 365 days for Medicare and TRICARE), the due dates of open appeal, records and refund tasks, and physician certifications expiring 60 days after signing for patients on scheduled trips; **Deadline Action** (**Deadline Actions** table) raises a task due within 14 days to priority 2 and within 3 days to 3, and opens a **Deadline Reminder** for timely filing within 30 days or passed and certifications within 14 days, once per claim and kind.
- **Facility invoicing**: a claim whose **Responsible Facility** is set (claim page, `claims.facility_id`; facilities under Admin) is on the `facility` step and waits; **Invoice Facilities** runs on the first of each month (in the agency's time zone) and issues one PDF per facility for the trips through last month with a balance that no invoice to that facility billed yet (`facility_invoice_claims`), so a trip assigned to a facility after its month was invoiced goes on the next, supplemental invoice (`facility_invoices.sequence`); each invoice is logged as `facility_invoiced` in each claim's files. A facility that is responsible for claims or has been invoiced cannot be deleted. The A/R aging report shows the facility as who owes the claim.

## Documents

The claim page Documents panel uploads a facesheet, PCS, signature form, ABN or MSP form (PDF, PNG, JPEG or TIFF) with its signed date; it is stored in S3 under `documents/<agency>/<claim>/`, kept in `claim_documents`, logged as a `document` event and starts **Receive Document**: **Document Facts** (Document Kinds table) puts a PCS or ABN on file with its date or signs the release for billing, the patch signals the claim so its cycle looks again, and Check Claim runs. **Chase Document** opens a task due in the days the **Document Chase Days** table gives, and asks again until a document of that kind is on file.
